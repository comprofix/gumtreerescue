## [1.0.0](https://github.com/comprofix/gumtreerescue/commits/v1.0.0) - 2026-10-30

The first release of Gum Tree Rescue: koalas fall from a gum tree, and
two rangers with a sheet bounce them across the paddock into the tray of
their ute.

### Added

- Bounce koalas from the tree to the ute: five zones on the sheet send a
  koala one or two steps back, straight up, or one or two steps on, and
  every bounce takes the same time.
- Easy, Normal and Hard skills, each with its own high score table. On
  Hard the landing markers show only at the last moment.
- Levels with new branches to drop from and a wobble before each drop;
  ten rescues end a level, and the ute drives off with them, then a tally.
  Past the level table the game goes on, a little faster each level.
- Drops are held back when the rangers could not reach them playing safe.
- An extra life every 5,000 points.
- Joeys from level 5, worth double. Koalas can grab the tree or slip from
  it, and lose their joeys, who climb back to them.
- Landing markers drawn as the koala's shadow, growing as it comes down.
- Dazed koalas, circled by mini kookaburras, after hitting the ute's
  headboard.
- The country bush: the gum tree, the rangers and their ute with rolling
  wheels, dust and smoke, background gums, birds that fly tree to tree,
  and kangaroos with joeys behind the fence.
- Two looks, Smooth and pixel art, switched in Options.
- The game's own tunes and sound effects: a bush band two-step, bird
  calls, the ute's engine and reverse beeper, and more.
- A demo of the rangers playing behind the title.
- The game's own koala icon.
- Play with the keys, a gamepad or the mouse.
- Downloads for Windows (zip) and Linux (tarball).
